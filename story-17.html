UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Code Geass

A clever student receives a strange tactical interface and must decide whether winning a conflict is worth the cost of manipulating others.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

Lelouch discovered the interface on an abandoned train platform. It projected a city map and predicted how crowds might move during a public emergency. The system could help people evacuate—or be used to control them.

When a power failure trapped commuters inside the station, Lelouch used the map to direct people toward safe exits. He shared the plan openly with station staff instead of keeping the advantage to himself.

The evacuation worked, though his predictions were imperfect. A child stopped to retrieve a toy, and the crowd had to adjust. Lelouch added the detail to the system, reminding himself that people were never pieces on a board.

He shut the interface down and chose to work with the community rather than command it from the shadows.
