UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure

A strange family heirloom reveals a hidden map, sending a curious traveler on a puzzle-filled journey through a house where nothing behaves normally.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

Every clock in the Joestar house stopped at 8:08. Jonathan found the only moving hand on a pocket watch passed down through his family. It pointed toward a portrait whose eyes seemed to follow him.

Behind the frame was a brass key and a note written in reverse. Jonathan used a mirror to read it, then followed clues through the conservatory, library, and old music room. Each puzzle required a different skill: memory, balance, or listening carefully.

The final lock opened a small room full of letters from relatives who had traveled the world. The heirloom was not a weapon or a treasure, but a record of people who had chosen curiosity over fear.

Jonathan reset the clocks. At 8:09, the house began ticking again, as if it approved of the answer.
