UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Naruto: Shippuden

Years of training lead a young ninja back to a changing village, where an urgent mission tests loyalty, patience, and the meaning of home.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

The wind carried the sound of training bells across the village rooftops. Kai returned after years away, taller and stronger, but the streets seemed smaller than he remembered.

Before he could greet his old friends, a messenger delivered a mission scroll marked with the symbol of a missing patrol. The team assembled at the eastern gate, each carrying memories they had never discussed.

Their trail ended in a ravine where a decoy had been left to distract them. Instead of charging ahead, Kai listened to the birds and noticed that their calls stopped near a hidden path. The team followed it and found the patrol safe, trapped by a landslide.

At sunset, Kai looked toward the village lights. Strength had brought him back, but trust had brought everyone home.
