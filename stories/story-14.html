UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Tokyo Ghoul

A student gets lost in a nighttime market where every stall hides a secret, and must learn to listen before judging those who seem frightening.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

Kaneki missed the last train and wandered into a market lit by red paper lamps. The vendors wore pale masks and spoke in whispers. Every stall offered something unusual: forgotten keys, old letters, or jars filled with rainwater.

A masked guide led him to a booth where a woman sorted lost belongings. She explained that the market gathered things people had abandoned without realizing their value. Kaneki found his own notebook among them, filled with sketches he thought he had lost forever.

When a sudden blackout scattered the crowd, Kaneki helped the vendors light their lamps and guide visitors to the exit. The masks looked less frightening in the warm light; behind them were tired, worried faces.

He caught the first train home carrying his notebook and a reminder: fear often grows where understanding has not yet begun.
