UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Mob Psycho 100

A quiet student with unusual abilities helps a neighborhood solve a series of harmless but baffling disturbances, learning that character matters more than power.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

Mob wanted an ordinary afternoon, but every umbrella on the shopping street had begun floating upside down. A crowd gathered as the umbrellas spun like a slow, colorful storm.

Instead of unleashing his powers, Mob asked the shopkeepers what had changed. They found a street performer’s magnetic prop hidden beneath a metal awning, pulling the umbrella frames upward whenever the device warmed.

Mob helped unplug it and returned the umbrellas to their owners. The performer apologized and offered to help clean the street. No one needed a dramatic battle, only a calm question and a willingness to fix the mistake.

On the walk home, Mob felt quietly proud. Becoming a better person was a kind of growth no power meter could measure.
