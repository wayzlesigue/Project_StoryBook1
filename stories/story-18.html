UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Steins;Gate

A student inventor notices that a homemade device is sending messages at impossible times, leading to a careful experiment about cause and consequence.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

Okabe’s homemade receiver printed a message dated tomorrow. At first, he celebrated the discovery, then realized the message warned him not to switch on the lab’s old power supply.

He gathered his friends and designed a safe test: unplug everything, record each event, and change only one variable at a time. The receiver’s clock was misconfigured, and a delayed network packet had made the message appear to come from the future.

The explanation was less dramatic than time travel, but the experiment taught them to respect evidence. They repaired the clock and saved their notes for the next project.

Okabe still called the lab a secret research institute. Now, however, he added one rule to its wall: wonder boldly, test carefully.
