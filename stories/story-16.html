UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Slam Dunk

A rough-around-the-edges basketball player learns that a team is built through practice, humility, and showing up for one another.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

The gym echoed with missed shots. Hanamichi had promised himself he would become the star of the team, but his first practice ended with more balls bouncing away than landing in the hoop.

A senior player asked him to stay after practice and work on passing drills. Hanamichi hated how ordinary the exercise felt, yet he kept returning each afternoon. Slowly, he learned to look up before dribbling and to trust the teammate moving into open space.

During a scrimmage, he passed instead of forcing a difficult shot. His teammate scored, and the bench erupted. For the first time, Hanamichi felt proud of a point he had not made himself.

The scoreboard still mattered, but now he understood the game was bigger than one name.
