Monster

A doctor notices a pattern linking several anonymous letters and begins a careful investigation into how ordinary choices can change lives.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

Dr. Kenji received the first letter in a plain envelope. It contained a hospital appointment card and one sentence: “You saved the wrong person.” The message disturbed him because the handwriting matched a note from years earlier.

Instead of chasing rumors, he reviewed his old records and spoke with people who remembered that difficult night. Each account revealed a different piece of the story, including a decision that had seemed small at the time.

The trail led to a retired archivist who had been protecting a witness from a false accusation. Kenji brought the evidence to the authorities and let the facts speak for themselves.

The final letter arrived weeks later, blank except for a date. Kenji filed it away. Not every mystery needed a dramatic answer; some needed patience, records, and a willingness to admit uncertainty.
