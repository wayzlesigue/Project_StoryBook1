UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Gintama

A group of odd-job workers accepts a simple delivery that turns into a citywide mix-up, proving that friendship can survive even the silliest chaos.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

Gintoki accepted a delivery job because it promised free snacks. The package was labeled “fragile,” “urgent,” and “do not open,” which naturally made everyone in the shop suspicious.

One wrong turn led them into a parade, where the package was mistaken for a festival prize. Kagura chased it through a noodle stall while Shinpachi tried to explain the situation to a marching band.

At last, they delivered the box to a tiny clockmaker. Inside was a replacement bell for the town clock, and the clockmaker paid them in snacks exactly as promised.

As the bell rang over the rooftops, Gintoki declared the mission a triumph. Nobody agreed on the details, but everyone agreed it had been a memorable afternoon.
