UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Haikyuu!!

A determined volleyball player and his teammates prepare for a tough match, discovering that timing and trust can turn a small team into a strong one.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

The gym lights buzzed above the net as Hinata practiced one more jump. His timing was fast, but the ball kept arriving just beyond his reach.

Instead of repeating the same drill, the team slowed down. Kageyama watched the rhythm of Hinata’s steps while the others called out the count. They adjusted by fractions of a second until the set and jump finally met in the air.

During the practice match, the opposing team blocked their favorite play. Hinata changed direction at the last moment and sent the ball to an open teammate. The point belonged to the whole court.

They left exhausted, laughing at their earlier mistakes. The next match would be difficult, but now they trusted the rhythm they had built together.
