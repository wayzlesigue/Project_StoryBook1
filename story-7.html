UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Bleach

A young guardian follows a trail of glowing paper lanterns through a hidden city, helping lost spirits find the memories that will guide them home.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

The first lantern appeared outside Ichiro’s window just after midnight. It floated above the street, glowing pale blue, then drifted toward an alley that had not been there the day before.

Beyond it lay a quiet city of bridges and silver trees. A small spirit waited beside a dry fountain, unable to remember the name of the person it sought. Ichiro followed the lanterns through empty markets, gathering little clues: a song, a paper star, a faded photograph.

When the spirit heard the song again, its memory returned—not as a face, but as the feeling of being loved. The lanterns rose into the sky, making a path of light.

Ichiro returned before dawn, understanding that protecting a soul sometimes meant helping it remember.
