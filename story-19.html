UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Cowboy Bebop

A crew of space travelers follows a mysterious distress signal and learns that not every lost person wants to be found the same way.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

The Bebop picked up a distress signal near a silent cargo station. Spike and the crew expected a stranded pilot, but found a small maintenance robot repeating an old message.

The robot’s logs showed that its owner had left voluntarily after sending the signal by mistake. The crew could collect the reward by reporting the location, or take time to find out whether the owner was safe.

They tracked the signal to a quiet moon settlement, where the pilot had started a new life repairing farm equipment. The crew delivered the robot and left without asking for a bounty.

Back aboard the Bebop, the stars looked as empty as ever, but the crew felt lighter. Not every mission ended with money. Some ended with a person getting another chance.
