UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Spy x Family

A carefully planned school event turns chaotic, and an unusual family must rely on honesty and teamwork to get through the day.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

Loid had planned every minute of the school fair. Yor was in charge of the food stall, Anya handled decorations, and Bond was meant to stay out of trouble.

Then a gust of wind scattered the invitation cards across the courtyard. Anya ran after them, Yor caught a falling banner, and Loid abandoned his perfect schedule to help the other families secure their booths.

The fair opened late, but the courtyard looked brighter than anyone expected. Neighbors shared snacks, children traded handmade badges, and even the strict headmaster stopped to admire the decorations.

On the walk home, the family agreed that the best plan was sometimes the one that left room for everyone to help.
