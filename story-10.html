UNOFFICIAL FAN STORY · CHAPTER 01

Vagabond

A wandering swordsman challenges his own pride after meeting a patient farmer who shows him a different kind of strength.

Chapter One: A New Beginning

The road curved through fields of tall grass, and Takezo walked it with his sword wrapped in cloth. He had won every duel that mattered to him, yet the victories felt strangely empty.

At a farm, an old caretaker asked him to mend a broken fence before accepting a meal. Takezo nearly refused, but the farmer handed him a hammer instead of a blade. The work was slow, awkward, and more exhausting than he expected.

For three days he helped repair the fence and learned how the farmer read weather, soil, and the moods of animals. No one praised him for it. Still, he began to notice how much strength it took to protect something fragile.

When he finally returned to the road, his sword remained wrapped. He had not become weaker; he had discovered another way to measure himself.
